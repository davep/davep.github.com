I'm still working on the [client certificate management
PR](https://github.com/davep/rogallo/pull/373) for
[Rogallo](https://rogallo.davep.dev/), which, of course, heavily leans on
the API of [Wasat](https://wasat.davep.dev/). In most cases, I've noticed
that something I want to do in the PR can already be done, but the work to
perform the action would seem like unnecessary detail in a client
application; I want to make using Wasat as frictionless as possible.

With this in mind, [v1.5.0](https://wasat.davep.dev/changelog/#v150) adds
interfaces for exporting and importing client certificate data.
