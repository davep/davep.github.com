Today was a wonderful day of fossil hunting on the east beach in
[Whitby](/tag/whitby/). We were ready to get down onto the beach before the
tide was out enough to let us on, and got off not too long before it would
have been back in again (but with more time to spare than the [last
time](/2026/04/20/whitby.html)). Fossils were found. Rock pools were
studied. Fun was had. Donuts and candy-floss were consumed on the way back
to the car.

It was a pretty ideal day.
