## Introduction

An occasional collection of things I've [recently
found](/series/recently-i-found/) on the Internet and added to [my
bookmarks](/bookmarks/).

## [The Russian Bot Collapse Report](https://russianbots.america2.news/)

Many people were talking about how lots of accounts went quiet when the
Russian data centres were attacked. Does the data support this?

## [TurboLisp](https://gitlab.com/patchwork1-ai/turbolisp)

A new Common Lisp development environment. I need to try this out at some
point.

## [What's getting pwned right now](https://pwn.watch/)

While the whole thing smells of being vibe-coded (I don't know if it is, but
it has that look/feel), this does seem like a handy thing to keep a link to.

## [SteamOS 3.9](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1675200/view/694275728822244963?l=english)

SteamOS 3.9. By the looks of things, this is the first version of SteamOS
that lets me hook the Steam Machine and the Steam Frame up together to play
some PCVR.

## [Story of Your Life](https://fyp.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/chiang_story_of_your_life.pdf)

A PDF of the short story that is the basis for the movie Arrival.

## [Emacs turns 50](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/10/20/1145399/emacs-turns-50/)

All my tools are getting old, just like me...

## [Why I Resist Generative "AI"](https://estherweidauer.com/articles/why-resist-generative-ai.html)

Pretty good essay on one person's reasons for resisting GenAI.

## [Frame Control](https://framecontrol.fun/)

Useful-looking application that lets you manage all sorts of aspects of your
Steam Frame, from a desktop/laptop.

## [No, Transformers Won't End the Human Race lol](https://www.stephendiehl.com/posts/transformers_wont_end_human_race/)

An essay on how a lot of the doom related to AI, and the impact it could
have on us, is tied up with hype about AI.

## [Manifestos, Fear, and "Loser Thinking"](https://www.404media.co/manifestos-fear-and-loser-thinking-inside-protons-ai-reckoning/)

## [Base](https://menial.co.uk/base/)

A macOS-native GUI for working with SQLite.

A good takedown of the current crop of "eh, X is kind of good again" noises
some people are making. This fits in well with the reasons why I nuked my
made-in-2007 account and why you'll never find me back on there ever again.
