Today I learned that [there's a version of XScreenSaver for
iOS](https://itunes.apple.com/app/xscreensaver/id539014593?mt=8). I haven't
used [XScreenSaver](https://www.jwz.org/xscreensaver/) in years; probably
decades at this point. Not because I don't like it or anything; it's just
because I've not had a GNU/Linux desktop machine in that long. But the
chance to install the app on my phone and scroll through all the different
graphics demos is too good to pass up.

![Tron Bit](/attachments/2026/10/21/bit.webp#centre)

I noticed this because I saw
[`xscreensaver-official/xscreensaver`](https://github.com/xscreensaver-official/xscreensaver)
pop up in my feed on GitHub.

![Digital Rain](/attachments/2026/10/21/rain.webp#centre)

I, of course, added a star to the repo [because the owner asked
nicely](https://www.jwz.org/blog/2026/10/gitsquatting/).

![Flying Toasters](/attachments/2026/10/21/toasters.webp#centre)

Now I might have to throw the Mac version on my Macs.

Or, you know, turn one of my older MacBooks into a Linux laptop and use it
there (something I keep meaning to do).
