Some might remember that, earlier this year, Valve made the Steam Machine
available for purchase. I'd had my eye on one since they were first
announced; I had it on my wishlist. Having had a lot of fun with my [Steam
Deck](/tag/steam-deck/), I was sure I'd appreciate what was looking to be
its bigger sibling (in terms of size and power, if not age).

Also, having had success with [buying the Steam
Controller](/series/buying-the-steam-controller/), I was all set to sign up
for the "lottery" and see if I could get into the queue for a Machine too.
But there was a wrinkle: the opening of the window to throw your hat into
the ring, and the projected delivery date should you be lucky, coincided
[with our holiday in the Netherlands](/2026/06/29/a-view-of-the-wash.html).
If I'd been successful, delivery would have taken place while I was out of
the country[^1].

Given this, I waited until I got back and then put my name down for a 512GB
machine without a controller. Unsurprisingly, I found myself on the
waitlist, not a reservation list. So I waited. And waited. And waited. Along
with a lot of other people.

Then, last week, I was scrolling through Reddit and saw a few people talking
about how they'd left the 512/no-controller list, jumped on the
512/with-controller list, and then got a purchase invitation email pretty
quickly; some within minutes, some within hours. After some wondering about
this, I decided it was worth a try. What's the worst that could happen? I
still have to wait, which I was anyway, or I get the offer to buy and I'm
down about an extra £80 because I went for a controller too.

I decided that, just for the laugh if nothing else, I'd go for it. I removed
myself from the 512-no-controller list, added myself to the
512-with-controller list, and... within minutes I had a purchase invitation
email! Oh shit! Now I have to decide!

So I decided.

Fast forward to this afternoon and...

![My own Steam Machine](/attachments/2026/10/08/steam-machine.webp#centre)

So, yeah, along with the [Steam Frame](/series/buying-the-steam-frame/),
that's my toy budget used up for a wee while to come!

I've not had the chance to unbox it yet and likely won't get the chance
until the weekend. While, yes, I could be doing it now rather than typing
this, I'm rattling this out between routine family duties and unboxing it
would not have given me the free time to actually have a proper play.

Once I do get the chance, I'll try and make a point of jotting down my
initial impressions.

Hopefully, at some point soon, I'll get to hook it up with the Steam Frame
too and see how well they work together. As I understand it, that's still a
bit of a "coming soon" thing, with work in progress on the SteamOS beta on
both hardware platforms.

So, at this point, I've somehow ended up with a reasonable little Steam
ecosystem:

- Steam Controller (MkII) (times two)
- Steam Deck
- Steam Frame
- Steam Machine

Also, if we're going to throw in Steam hardware of old, I even still own a
[Steam Link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steam_Link). At this point, I
kind of wish I had my hands on a [first generation Steam
Controller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steam_Controller_(1st_generation)),
just to be somewhat completionist.

[^1]: Ironically, while in the country where the device would ship from.
