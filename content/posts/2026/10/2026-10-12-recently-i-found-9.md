## Introduction

An occasional collection of things I've [recently
found](/series/recently-i-found/) on the Internet and added to [my
bookmarks](/bookmarks/).

## [DHH Is Way Worse Than I Thought](https://jakelazaroff.com/words/dhh-is-way-worse-than-i-thought/)

While a year old at the point of saving this, it's still a pretty good
collection of details of problematic things related to DHH. It's only got
worse since.

## [Awesome Steam Frame](https://github.com/kosantosbik/awesome-steam-frame)

An "awesome" list of all sorts of things related to the [Steam
Frame](/tag/steam-frame/).

## [Mach Run](https://jebkj21.itch.io/mach-run)

Fun arcade-style flying game that runs well in the browser. A great way to
kill 10 minutes.

## [Hole Punch](https://notoriousbfg.com/hole-punch/)

A web-based gravity puzzle game.

## [Gravity Assist](https://www.michaelfogleman.com/gravity-assist/#slingshot)

Another web-based gravity puzzle game.

## [Getting Started With XR in Godot 4.3 Tutorial!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BrNZs4XzU0w)

A video tutorial that goes into the basics of getting some VR code going in
Godot.

## [R U STILL THERE?](https://refukked.lovable.app/)

How well are Reform folk doing after they got jobs they didn't know they had
to work at?

## [You are a tornado](https://jasperblank.itch.io/you-are-a-tornado)

There's plenty of tornado-chasing games out there. Quite a lot of them.
Games where you are the tornado... not so many. Here's one.

## [If you accept something is unethical, its usefulness is irrelevant, isn't it?](https://hachyderm.io/@jzb/117004026218658407)

A good toot-sized argument against the "both sides balanced" take on the
current state of AI use. Or, rather, it's a good argument in favour of the
"it's unethical so I won't use it" approach not being stupidly stubborn.

## [Why is Hacker News like that?](https://drewdevault.com/blog/Why-is-HN-like-that/)

Good essay that goes over the general political feel of Hacker News, and
goes into the background of why it is that way.

## [Free Promotions on Steam](https://steamdb.info/upcoming/free/)

A list of stuff that is currently free on Steam.
