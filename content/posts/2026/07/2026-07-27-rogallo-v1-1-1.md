I've just made a small bug-fix release for
[Rogallo](https://rogallo.davep.dev/).
[v1.1.1](https://rogallo.davep.dev/changelog/#v111) fixes two (mostly)
cosmetic issues [that were reported by a
user](https://github.com/davep/rogallo/discussions/227). From the ChangeLog:

- Fixed `cosy_link_jumps` not being loaded from configuration when Rogallo
  starts up. ([#230](https://github.com/davep/rogallo/pull/230))
- Fixed viewer status line being lost when `maximum_document_width` is set
  to something other than `0`.
  ([#231](https://github.com/davep/rogallo/pull/231))

Thanks to [fab](gemini://redterminal.org/)[^1] for alerting me to these issues.

One other small change in this release is that the version of
[Port79](https://port79.davep.dev/) is included in the output from the
`diagnostics` CLI command.

[^1]: See [here](https://redterminal.org) if you don't have a Gemini client.
