Another quick little update to [Wasat](https://wasat.davep.dev/), bumping
the version to [v1.8.0](https://wasat.davep.dev/changelog/#v180). This
version is in support of some work I'm about to do on
[Rogallo](https://rogallo.davep.dev/): Titan support.

This release adds `TitanURI` and makes a number of changes to handle such
URIs alongside `GeminiURI`. A lucky side-effect is that a problem was
uncovered and corrected with how Gemini URIs are sliced and diced in the
library. This first showed up for me when [I was having a look at the
Scriptonite examples](https://github.com/davep/rogallo/issues/407). None of
them were working for no obvious reason. Eventually it became clear that
parts of the URIs were being lost as they travelled through Wasat's code.

So v1.8.0 clears up that problem (fixing the bug in Rogallo by simply
upgrading the library).

As of now, I can move on to [actually adding Titan
support](https://github.com/davep/rogallo/issues/355).
