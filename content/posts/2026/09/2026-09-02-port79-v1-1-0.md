A small update to [Port79](https://port79.davep.dev/), bumping up to
[v1.1.0](https://port79.davep.dev/changelog/#v110). This release adds a
`resolve` method to the `FingerURI` class.

I'm currently tweaking [Rogallo](https://rogallo.davep.dev/)'s rendering of
some responses from finger services and being able to resolve URIs relative
to the underlying page is going to be very helpful.
