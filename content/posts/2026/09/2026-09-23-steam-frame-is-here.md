Actually a little sooner than I was expecting -- look what turned up today!

![Steam Frame box](/attachments/2026/09/23/frame-box.webp#centre)

When I saw this morning, based on tracking, that it had made it to the
national distribution centre down in England, I was expecting it to head up
this way today and perhaps be out for delivery tomorrow. But, no, a little
later in the morning I got an email to say it was up here and would head out
this afternoon.

![Steam Frame box open](/attachments/2026/09/23/frame-open.webp#centre)

I probably won't be giving it a spin this evening, mainly because I had
plans to [stream some more
Atomfall](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLcJDuHvZlXzU) and I'm
actually really into the story right now. The other reason is that I'm still
waiting for the charger and ergonomic pack to show up. Not that I *need*
them (it's not like I don't have a charger that'll work), but it would be
nice to have those extra items to hand when I first have a play.

Realistically, I'm probably not going to get the chance to fire it up until
Friday evening. That's fine, I'm in no rush. I've gone this long without
playing much VR; a couple more evenings won't hurt.

Meanwhile, I've found out that [Gunman Contracts - Stand
Alone](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2421750/Gunman_Contracts__Stand_Alone/)
will actually work in standalone mode on the Frame. Yes, I know the name
might suggest that, but the "Stand Alone" in the name is in reference to the
fact that it's [no longer an Alyx
mod](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2600534901).
This makes me quite happy. This is a game I've been looking forward to
playing for a couple of years now and the prospect of being able to dive
into it on the [Steam Frame](/tag/steam-frame/), without needing to faff
with my PC, very much appeals.

I'll be sure to jot down some impressions once I've had a proper session.
