I didn't have to spend too long in the [Steam Frame](/tag/steam-frame/)
[reservation queue](/2026/09/18/in-the-steam-frame-queue.html). I'd just sat
down on the sofa, after having dinner yesterday evening, opened the MacBook
to hack on some code, and had this turn up:

![Purchase confirmation email](/attachments/2026/09/19/purchase-confirmation.webp#centre)

I didn't hesitate. However, during the checkout journey, there was a glitch.
I opened the email, clicked on the link to go and confirm the purchase, and
on the [Steam](/tag/steam/) site I was greeted with a message to the effect
that the quantity I was buying was too great! It wouldn't let me proceed to
the payment. I clicked on the button to view my basket and this is what I
saw:

![Two Frames for the price of two](/attachments/2026/09/19/two-frames-for-the-price-of-two.webp#centre)

This did make me slightly nervous for a moment. It was an obvious glitch,
but was it double-counting the same unit, or had it somehow added two units
to my basket? Given that I couldn't progress without doing something, I
removed one and... it was all okay.

So as of right now I'm paid up, I have an active order, and now I wait for
physical delivery. The estimated delivery time seems to be between 3 and 5
(working?) days.

Meanwhile, I now need to figure out what to do about prescription lens
inserts. The inserts that Valve recommend on [the Steam Frame
page](https://store.steampowered.com/hardware/steamframe) [are currently
"coming
soon"](https://www.zennioptical.com/steamframe-vr-prescription-insert),
which is mildly annoying. I have noticed in the [Steam Frame
subreddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/SteamFrame/) a couple of people
mentioning other companies who are already manufacturing and shipping
inserts. I might take a look at them.

The need for prescription lenses is one annoyance of [VR](/tag/vr/) for me.
Sure, it's easily fixed, but it's a bit of a shame when, upon opening the
box, I have to go with a blurry experience for a wee while. Of course, I
*could* wear my glasses in the headset, but based on my experience when I
got my Quest 2 back in late 2020, that's not comfortable *and* there's the
danger of scratching the glasses, headset lenses, or both.

It's not worth the risk.
