It looks like things are moving a wee bit quicker than I remember when I
[bought the Steam Controller](/series/buying-the-steam-controller/), with

![Steam Frame packaged](/attachments/2026/09/20/steam-frame-packaged.webp#centre)

I'm curious as to when it will arrive. The small number of times that I've
purchased Valve hardware, I've generally found the actual shipping (once
it's shipped) to be reasonably swift.

I could be in the position of being able to play with my new headset before
next weekend. While I'm most interested in the standalone experience, I
*might* have to dig out the [VR gaming
PC](/2022/05/28/my-vr-recording-setup.html), update a bunch of things on it,
and give it a general tidy. It's getting on a bit now, but it will be
interesting to test out the wireless PCVR mode.
