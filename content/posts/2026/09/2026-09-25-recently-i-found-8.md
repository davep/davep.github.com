## Introduction

An occasional collection of things I've [recently
found](/series/recently-i-found/) on the Internet and added to [my
bookmarks](/bookmarks/).

## [Weird little guys of FOSS](https://drewdevault.com/weird-guys/)

As the name says: a collection of "weird little guys" of the FOSS and
adjacent worlds. Massively subjective, of course, but I don't think I've
seen one (that I know of) that I disagree with yet.

## [Ball Splitter](https://danieljackson-oslo.github.io/balls/)

The sort of thing that's fun to run up, muted, during boring meetings.

## [Deadpan Photography: Enjoying the Pretence](https://photoni.st/index.php/2026/07/12/deadpan-photography-enjoying-the-pretence/)

I love this style of photography, but I don't think I've ever seen an actual
name for the style before. Now I have.

## [Stargate Worlds — Fan-Made Private Server](https://thefifthrace.online/)

I don't need another game to invest all my time in at the moment. But if I
did, I think it could be this. I love all things Stargate.

## [Geminispace topology](https://clehaxze.tw/misc/geminispace-topology-2026-09/)

A map of Geminispace.

## [Kepler protocol specification](https://github.com/kevinboone/kepler-protocol)

Yet another smolweb protocol, and yet another derived from or related to
Gemini. I doubt I'll do anything with this one (Rogallo supports enough as
it is for now), but it's worth keeping a link to it.

## [GitHub star history](https://star-history.dera.page/)

A GitHub star history that doesn't need access tokens and the like.

## [Everybody's Lost Their Minds](https://www.netmeister.org/blog/everybodys-lost-their-minds.html)

A pretty great rant about all things AI.

## [Flet](https://flet.dev/)

I build enough applications using Textual; it might be interesting to play
with a framework that works on all sorts of platforms (as opposed to just in
the terminal).

## [Great on Frame](https://store.steampowered.com/greatonframe?curator_clanid=45479024)

Games that are verified to work standalone on the [Steam Frame](/tag/steam-frame/).

## [Leaving DigitalOcean, one site at a time](https://keith.is/post/leaving-digitalocean/)

I've not used DO for a while now, but I have been thinking about setting up
a small host again so I can play with Gemini and Gopher. Having used DO in
the past, they were at the top of my list.

Now I'm not so sure I need them there.

## [endoflife.date](https://endoflife.date/)

End-of-life dates for... so many things!

## [Team Fortress 2: VR Mod](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1055600/Team_Fortress_2_VR_Mod/)

I used to have so much fun with TF2. The idea of TF2 in VR... hell yes
please!
