## Introduction

An occasional collection of things I've [recently
found](/series/recently-i-found/) on the Internet and added to [my
bookmarks](/bookmarks/).

## [Outcrop](https://outcrop.rocks/)

A geology app that shows the geologic map wherever you are, draws it onto
the land in front of you in augmented reality, and works offline. I've yet
to give it a go, but I can see it being an interesting thing to play with on
my next visit to [Whitby](/tag/whitby/).

## [Building a Gemini server in Python](https://willmunn.xyz/gemini/python/2026/10/16/we-have-lift-off.html)

Someone's journey building a Gemini protocol server in Python.

## [Anytext](https://github.com/davidbrochart/anytext)

A hard fork of Textual. There are some deviations, but it's very much worth
keeping an eye on because a) it's being actively maintained (unlike Textual
itself) and b) it will also allow building applications that run inside a
web page.

## [Who wants to fuck with Elon Musk?](https://www.cardsagainsthumanity.com/elonhasnofriends)

An effort to build a monument to Elon Musk.

## [Anatomy of Apple's Personal Knowledge Graph](https://8rank.ai/game/apple-intelligence-personal-knowledge-graph)

A post that goes into detail about Apple's personal knowledge graph, and how
to access the content on macOS.

## [Psychological Reactions to Subordinates' LLM Use at Work](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1048984326000639)

Quote: *"The present research uncovers a novel, robust effect: subordinate
LLM use decreases supervisory trust. The effect is consistent across
contexts, task criticality, and supervisors’ prior LLM experience, and is
only eliminated when LLM use at work is specifically encouraged. This work
informs leadership theory and practice and extends the emerging literature
on human-AI interaction at work. While subordinates’ LLM use involves
benefits and downsides for subordinates and the organizations in which they
work, their supervisors may view such use largely negatively."*

## [Rust Style Guide](https://google.github.io/styleguide/rustguide.html)

## [Abyssal Garden](https://noniv.github.io/abyssal-garden/)

## [Suicide of a Nation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iL3gpxD6_rs)

Shaun takes on a questionable book, with a fascinating side quest related to
using AI/LLMs as writing aids. While there's plenty of interesting political
content here, I think there's also a lesson for the adoption of LLMs
internally within businesses -- one they will either never learn, or will
learn too late.
