Outcrop¶

A geology app that shows the geologic map wherever you are, draws it onto the land in front of you in augmented reality, and works offline. I've yet to give it a go, but I can see it being an interesting thing to play with on my next visit to Whitby.

Anytext¶

A hard fork of Textual. There are some deviations, but it's very much worth keeping an eye on because a) it's being actively maintained (unlike Textual itself) and b) it will also allow building applications that run inside a web page.

Anatomy of Apple's Personal Knowledge Graph¶

Psychological Reactions to Subordinates' LLM Use at Work¶

Quote: "The present research uncovers a novel, robust effect: subordinate LLM use decreases supervisory trust. The effect is consistent across contexts, task criticality, and supervisors’ prior LLM experience, and is only eliminated when LLM use at work is specifically encouraged. This work informs leadership theory and practice and extends the emerging literature on human-AI interaction at work. While subordinates’ LLM use involves benefits and downsides for subordinates and the organizations in which they work, their supervisors may view such use largely negatively."
