Introduction¶

An occasional collection of things I've recently found on the Internet and added to my bookmarks.

Weird little guys of FOSS¶

As the name says: a collection of "weird little guys" of the FOSS and adjacent worlds. Massively subjective, of course, but I don't think I've seen one (that I know of) that I disagree with yet.

Ball Splitter¶

The sort of thing that's fun to run up, muted, during boring meetings.

Deadpan Photography: Enjoying the Pretence¶

I love this style of photography, but I don't think I've ever seen an actual name for the style before. Now I have.

Stargate Worlds — Fan-Made Private Server¶

I don't need another game to invest all my time in at the moment. But if I did, I think it could be this. I love all things Stargate.

Geminispace topology¶

Kepler protocol specification¶

Yet another smolweb protocol, and yet another derived from or related to Gemini. I doubt I'll do anything with this one (Rogallo supports enough as it is for now), but it's worth keeping a link to it.

GitHub star history¶

Everybody's Lost Their Minds¶

Flet¶

Great on Frame¶

Games that are verified to work standalone on the Steam Frame.

Leaving DigitalOcean, one site at a time¶

I've not used DO for a while now, but I have been thinking about setting up a small host again so I can play with Gemini and Gopher. Having used DO in the past, they were at the top of my list.

Now I'm not so sure I need them there.
